Getting married at Tambo del Inka: what to know for your photos and film

By Albaside Studio · November 15, 2026

Leer en español: Casarte en Tambo del Inka

An outdoor ceremony in the Sacred Valley, with the mountains as a backdrop.

Short answer: Tambo del Inka is a Luxury Collection resort in Urubamba, on the banks of the Urubamba River, in the heart of the Sacred Valley. It has riverside gardens for outdoor ceremonies, several halls for the reception and even its own train station to Machu Picchu. For photo and film, what matters most is choosing the ceremony time well and leaving room for portraits with the mountains behind you.

Why so many couples choose Tambo del Inka

Couples who search for Tambo del Inka are usually already picturing a Sacred Valley wedding: mountains all around, the river next to you and a hotel that houses your guests in the same place. That makes planning much simpler, especially for a destination wedding where almost everyone is travelling in.

For a photo and film team, a venue like this has a big advantage: everything happens close together. Getting ready, ceremony, cocktail hour and party can be a few minutes' walk apart, which means more time photographing you and less time in transit.

Light: choosing your ceremony time

The midday sun in the valley is strong and leaves hard shadows on faces. If your ceremony is in the gardens, we recommend scheduling it in the afternoon so your couple portraits land in the softer light before sunset.

  • Outdoor ceremony: mid-afternoon works better than midday. If it has to be early, we plan where you stand so the sun is not in your eyes.
  • Couple portraits: set aside 30 to 40 minutes close to sunset, with the river and mountains behind you.
  • Party: we bring our own lighting for the first dance and the dance floor without changing the atmosphere you designed.

Spots on the property that photograph beautifully

The riverside gardens, the paths with mountain views and the hotel's wood and stone interiors give plenty of variety without leaving the venue. The hotel's own train station is a very special backdrop, but it is always arranged in advance with the hotel and around the train schedule.

Logistics to sort out early

  • Transfers: from Cusco airport it is about 1 hour 25 minutes by car. If guests arrive the same day, leave buffer time in the schedule.
  • Altitude: Urubamba sits lower than the city of Cusco, which helps guests acclimatise. Still, arriving a day or two early is a good idea.
  • Season: May to September is the dry season in the valley; November to March is rainier, so we always plan an indoor backup.
  • Coordination: the hotel has its own wedding planners. We coordinate with them so the timeline gives photo and film moments the right place.

Frequently asked questions

From Cusco airport it is about 1 hour 25 minutes by car, depending on traffic and route.

Can we take photos at the hotel train station?

It is arranged in advance with the hotel and around the train schedule.
