The best Cusco locations for your pre-wedding photos

By Albaside Studio · November 10, 2026

Leer en español: Los mejores lugares de Cusco para tu sesión pre boda

The towers of Cusco Cathedral from a viewpoint above the city.

Short answer: the places that work best for pre-wedding photos in Cusco are the Plaza de Armas (with the Cathedral and the Compañía de Jesús), the San Blas neighborhood, the Inca stone streets of the historic center and the viewpoints above the city. Shoot early in the morning or at sunset.

We have photographed pre-wedding sessions in Cusco with Peruvian and international couples, and we always look for the same thing: photos that could only exist here. These are the places we use most, and how to make the most of them.

1. Plaza de Armas: the Cathedral and the Compañía de Jesús

The heart of Cusco and its most recognizable backdrop. The Compañía de Jesús at sunset, as the square's lights come on, is one of our favorite shots. It gets busy, so go very early for an empty square, or late afternoon for atmosphere and warm light.

2. San Blas

The artisans' quarter has steep streets, wooden balconies and a panoramic view of the historic center. It works beautifully at sunset and is perfect for relaxed, walking-hand-in-hand photos.

3. Streets with Inca walls

The historic center's streets are lined with perfectly fitted Inca stonework. They make richly textured backdrops, ideal for black-and-white or soft-light portraits. Narrow passages give even shade even at midday.

4. Viewpoints over the city

From the San Cristóbal and Cristo Blanco viewpoints you see the tiled roofs and church towers. They are ideal for a wide shot that says "we got married in Cusco".

5. Colonial doors and courtyards

Carved wooden doors and colonial courtyards are perfect for intimate photos, with the dress spread out and the veil moving.

When to shoot

  • Time of day: sunrise in Cusco is roughly 5:30 to 6:30 am, and sunset roughly 4:30 to 6:00 pm depending on the season.
  • Season: the dry season (May to September) brings clear skies. The rainy season (November to March) is greener but needs a plan B.
  • Altitude: Cusco sits above 3,300 meters (about 11,000 feet). If you are flying in, arrive a day or two early and do not schedule the session on landing day.
About permits: some archaeological sites and church interiors have their own rules for professional photography. Before choosing one, confirm the current requirements with Peru's Ministry of Culture or the site's management.
