Getting married in the Sacred Valley: a practical guide

By Albaside Studio · November 10, 2026

Leer en español: Casarte en el Valle Sagrado: guía práctica

An outdoor ceremony with the mountains as witnesses.

Short answer: the Sacred Valley is one of the most beautiful places in Peru to get married. Choose the dry season (May to September) for an outdoor ceremony, always have a covered plan B, and plan transfers from Cusco early. For photo and film, the last hour of light among the mountains is gold.

We have covered weddings at Sacred Valley haciendas and hotels, and every one confirms the same thing: the landscape does half the work, but logistics decide whether the day flows. This guide gathers what we always discuss with couples who choose the valley.

Why the Sacred Valley

  • Scenery: mountains, farmland and the Urubamba River as the backdrop to your whole wedding.
  • Milder weather: the valley is lower than Cusco city, so it usually feels warmer and altitude affects guests less.
  • Space: haciendas, hotels and country houses with large gardens for ceremony, cocktail and party in one place. Some valley hotels, such as Tambo del Inka, have dedicated event areas.

The season matters

In the Cusco region the dry season runs May to September: sunny days, cold nights, clear skies. The rainy season runs November to March, with frequent afternoon showers; the valley is at its greenest, but an outdoor ceremony needs a plan B.

Always a plan B: even in the dry season, confirm your venue has a tent or covered space. It lets us plan where to take group photos if the weather turns.

Transfers from Cusco

The valley is a road trip from Cusco city. If guests stay in Cusco, organize round-trip transport and remember the return will be at night. Many couples have guests stay in the valley so everyone can enjoy the party.

Planning your photo and film

  1. Ceremony in good light: if it is outdoors, avoid harsh midday sun. Mid-afternoon usually gives more even light.
  2. Save 20 to 30 minutes for couple portraits close to sunset, when the mountains take on color.
  3. The cocktail hour counts: it is the most relaxed moment, perfect for candid photos with guests and for the film.
  4. Family photo list prepared in advance, so no light is lost looking for the missing aunt.

For couples traveling from abroad

If you or your guests come from overseas, we recommend arriving in the region two or three days before the wedding to acclimatize. We have worked with many international couples and coordinate in English or Spanish.
